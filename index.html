May – Oct 2026 · TABConf Lightning

The Summer Lightning Broke (a Little)

Every Lightning implementation shipped security fixes in summer 2026. When Core Lightning asked operators to go offline until a fix shipped, a fifth of CLN nodes went dark overnight, ten times an ordinary day. The rest of the network barely noticed: a Tor DoS wave a month earlier cost it twice as much, and one hub outweighs the whole CLN cohort. Measured from daily gossip and every on-chain close.
