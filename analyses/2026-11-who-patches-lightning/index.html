Who patches the Lightning Network?

A short guide to our paper on how Lightning nodes upgrade, and the data behind it.

  • The paper (arXiv)
  • Code and data
  • Companion: does client diversity protect Lightning?

Summary in plain words

Nodes patch on release and platform schedules, not on security advisories.

  • 1A node's release line can be read from the feature bits it announces. Where CLN nodes state their version, the inferred line is exact for 132 of 136 node–line pairs.
  • 2Half of LND nodes move to a new release line within 88 days. Small nodes and nodes already behind lag.
  • 3The three largest LND upgrade waves came about a day after Umbrel shipped the new version, 19–41 days after LND's own release.
  • 4Fixes ship a median 256 days before the advisory. Of 12 disclosures we could test, only CLN's of July 2026 sped up upgrades.
  • 5Two months after Lightning Labs' August batch, 71% of exposed LND nodes still ran an affected release. CLN's offline call was followed mostly by nodes already up to date.

1 A node's release line can be read from gossip

We read the default, optional and removable feature bits of every LND, CLN, Eclair and LDK release since 2019 from source. Releases with identical defaults form a release line (LND has 10). A node's announced bits identify its line; patch releases within a line are invisible.

Liveness matters. Of 2,689 announced LND nodes on lines older than 0.19, only 434 were live: 12.5% of live LND nodes, not the 42.3% a raw count gives.

2 Most operators upgrade within months; small and lagging nodes are slowest

Following each node from release day, half of LND nodes reached the new line within 88 days and 76% within a year (CLN: 137 days). A node more than one line behind has about five times lower odds of upgrading in any week.

3 Platforms set the pace

No large LND upgrade wave came on LND's release day. The three largest, 262–385 nodes each, followed Umbrel shipping LND 0.19.1, 0.20.0 and 0.21.1, 19–41 days after LND's release. At least 87% of each wave re-signed its announcement within 48 hours of Umbrel's merge.

4 Advisories come late, and move few of the nodes still behind

For 12 disclosures we compared the weekly rate at which nodes left affected lines in the 8 weeks before and the 4 weeks after (1 = no change). Only CLN's of 19 July 2026 clearly raised it, and only among nodes one line behind.

Intervals are wide: a ratio near 1 rules out a big response, not a small one. Moves right after platform releases are left out, and the "one line behind" split was chosen after seeing the data.

5 Most exposed LND nodes stayed exposed

At Lightning Labs' 11 August disclosure, 14.9% of live LND nodes (6.4% of live LND capacity) ran an affected line. Two months later, 71% of them still did. CLN's exposure fell further, but much of that fall was nodes leaving.

Who answered CLN's call to go offline

Mostly operators already up to date. Of the live nodes on the newest CLN line the day before the 26 August call, 23.4% were no longer live two days later, against 11.0% on older lines.

Takeaways

  • The platform's schedule is part of the patch window. A fix reaches most small nodes when their platform ships it.
  • Platform prompts reach laggards; advisories reach those already keeping up.
  • Exposure can be measured before disclosure. Maintainers can read, in aggregate, how many live nodes and how much capacity sit on an affected line.
  • Beyond Lightning (the paper's reading): wherever packagers stand between a project and its users, the packager's schedule likely sets the patch level.

Common misreadings

  • "71% of Lightning nodes are vulnerable."71% of the exposed LND nodes, which were 14.9% of live LND nodes and 6.4% of live LND capacity.
  • "Exposed nodes can be exploited."Exposed means running an affected line. Several bugs also need a channel with the victim.
  • "Advisories don't work."The tests rule out a big response, not a small one, and fixing first works for operators who follow releases.
  • "Only patched nodes went offline."Both groups did, at 23.4% and 11.0%.

How we measured it

  • Data: 814 daily gossip snapshots from our own node, May 2023 to 13 October 2026.
  • Live nodes only: a peer keeps an enabled channel toward the node, updated in the last 14 days. One managed fleet, which peaked at 1,781 live nodes, is left out.
  • Platforms: Umbrel releases dated to the minute from its public app store, and BTCPay's 2.4.2.
  • Advisories: 30 public LND and CLN advisories whose fix boundary gossip can see.

What this doesn't show

  • Patch releases within a line, including all of CLN's 2026 fixes.
  • Ground truth for LND, which never states its version; LND rests on consistency checks.
  • Other platforms (Start9, RaspiBlitz, myNode, hosted services), so platform shares are lower bounds.
  • Private nodes, including most mobile wallets.
