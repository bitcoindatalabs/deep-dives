Lightning Security On-chain May – October 2026 · TABConf 2026

The Summer Lightning Broke (a Little)

Every Lightning implementation shipped security fixes in the same ten weeks — but the bugs were different. We measured how the network absorbed them: one client cohort went dark on command, peers force-closed on it, most of it came back. Everything below comes from daily gossip snapshots and our own Bitcoin node.
